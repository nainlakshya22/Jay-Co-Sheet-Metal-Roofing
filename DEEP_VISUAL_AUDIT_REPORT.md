## 1. Visual Design & Theme Execution

### 1.1 Color Harmony & Consistency
- **Evaluation**: The three-color theme (Mocha Brown `#5B4632`, Champagne Gold `#F4D9A0`, Burnt Amber `#C8792C`) is applied consistently without overuse or color clutter.
- **Surface Hierarchy**:
  - Deep Mocha (`#120E0B`) serves as the dark canvas that eliminates glare.
  - Intermediate Mocha (`#1B1511`) creates elevated modular cards.
  - Champagne Gold (`#F4D9A0`) provides warm, non-harsh contrast for editorial titles and fine borders.
  - Burnt Amber (`#C8792C`) is strategically reserved for primary action points, buttons, and active filter states.
- *Status*: **PASS**

### 1.2 Typography & Heading Hierarchy
- **Font Stack**:
  - Headers: Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond serif with tracking (`tracking-tight`, `font-serif`).
  - Technical Data & Metadata: Monospace font for permits, dimensions, GAF credentials, and price points.
  - Body Text: Clean geometric sans-serif with high readability on dark backgrounds (`text-stone-300`).
- **Heading Scale**:
  - `H1`: 48px – 60px (Hero, major page headers)
  - `H2`: 32px – 40px (Section titles, division headers)
  - `H3`: 20px – 24px (Sub-features, card headers)
  - `H4`: 16px – 18px (Metric labels, checklist titles)
- *Status*: **PASS**

### 1.3 Imagery & Photographic Art Direction
- **Photographic Quality**:
  - High-resolution architectural photography featuring clean standing seam metal profiles, genuine hand-soldered copper dormers, natural slate roofs, and heavy bending brakes at the 2307 Knight St facility.
  - Warm golden-hour grading matches the reference aesthetic without using unrelated lifestyle or stock model imagery.
- *Status*: **PASS**
